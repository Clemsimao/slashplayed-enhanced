# iPad gameplay previews

These GIFs come from gameplay recordings supplied by the fork maintainer on November 11, 2026.
They show the actual iPad interface, not a rendered mockup.

- `ipad-gameplay.gif`: two 4.5-second excerpts, starting at 00:05 in the 01:40 recording and
  00:31 in the 01:42 recording. Both retain the full game view, scaled to 720 pixels wide at 8 frames/second.
- `touch-layout-editor.gif`: an eight-second excerpt starting at 00:36 in the 01:40 recording,
  cropped around the native editor and action buttons, then scaled to 800 pixels wide at 8 frames/second.
- `npc-quest-interaction.gif`: a six-second excerpt starting at 00:52.5 in the 02:00 recording,
  showing quest turn-in and acceptance, with the full game view scaled to 800 pixels wide at 10 frames/second.
- `enhanced-settings.gif`: a ten-second excerpt starting at 00:03 in the 01:56 recording,
  cropped around the native settings interface and scaled to 760 pixels wide at 8 frames/second.

Gameplay chat is blurred where present. The GIFs loop, have no audio, and contain no source-video metadata.
The original recordings are kept outside the repository. World of Warcraft and its game artwork
belong to Blizzard Entertainment; these recordings illustrate the fork's interface in use.
